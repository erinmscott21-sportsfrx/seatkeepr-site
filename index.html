The problem

Groups buy once a year. Then everyone forgets.

The person who organizes the company picnic or the youth league outing hears nothing for nine months. Your reps have hundreds of past groups and time to call a fraction of them. By the time the presale opens, plenty of groups have already made other plans.

Groups go quiet

Reps call the biggest groups first. Smaller groups never hear from anyone and simply don't come back.

Contacts move on

The HR coordinator who booked the outing changed jobs in March. Nobody notices until the date is gone and the group is too.

The perfect night goes unsold

Scout Night, Faith Night, a party deck with room for 60. The groups who'd love them never hear about them in time.

What it does

Remember. Notice. Match.

  1. Remember

    Every group from last season

    Who came, how many, which night, and what they loved or asked about, pulled from your bookings and your reps' notes into one ranked board.

  2. Notice

    When a contact has moved on

    Bounced emails, a new name on the account, a note that the organizer left. Seatkeepr flags it early, while there's still time to find the group's new organizer.

  3. Match

    Each group to the right offer

    Youth leagues to Kids Run the Bases. Churches to Faith Night. A company milestone to the party deck. Every match comes from your promo calendar and what you have left to sell.

What a rep sees

The call they'd make if they remembered every group.

Each group gets a clear next move: the night that fits, why it fits, and a ready-to-edit message telling the organizer about the presale before the public hears.

How it works

Last season in. A rebooking plan out.

  1. Step 1

    Connect what you have

    Read-only access to Salesforce, or just upload last season's group list. Add your promo calendar and group spaces.

  2. Step 2

    Get your rebooking board

    Every past group, ranked by who to call first, with contact changes flagged and the best night or space for each.

  3. Step 3

    Keep every group in the loop

    Presale and event alerts go out under your rep's name. Reps review the messages that matter most, so no group goes a season without hearing from you.
